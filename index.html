Help & Feedback
How many can I generate per day?

3 free generations per day, resetting at midnight. Each generation can be a full notes sheet or specimen paper.

Why did a section say "couldn't be generated"?

Rarely, all AI providers fail for one section. Try regenerating — it usually works on a second attempt.

How do I download as PDF?

Tap "Download as PDF" under any generated document — your browser's print dialog will open, where you can choose "Save as PDF".
